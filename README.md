Wireless charging to EV

The proposed wireless charging systems based on Arduino microcontroller represent a significant 
advancement in EV technology. 

• Uses electromagnetic induction for wireless energy transfer, eliminating the need for physical connectors.

• Primary coil (base unit) generates a magnetic field when powered. 

• Secondary coil (in vehicle) captures the magnetic field and converts it into electrical energy. 

• Enables automatic charging when the vehicle is positioned over the base coil. 

• Enhances EV adoption by improving convenience, efficiency, and sustainability in transportation systems.
